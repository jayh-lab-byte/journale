DAY 03 — We Got Lost in Berlin

A spontaneous detour into vinyl warmth, damp cobblestones, and sunlit canal courtyards.

PLATE 03A
Pariser Platz · 14:18 PM · 35mm Natural Daylight Archive Ref: DE-BER-0348

길을 잘못 들어선 건 오후 두 시쯤이었다. 브란덴부르크 문을 보고 다음 장소로 향하려 했지만, 한동안 방향을 찾지 못했다. 하지만 낯선 골목 끝에서 만난 작은 LP 카페와 따뜻한 햇살은 이번 여행에서 가장 오랫동안 기억될 순간이었다.

It was around two in the afternoon when our planned route dissolved. Leaving the neoclassical columns behind, the yellow tram carried us south toward the willow-draped bends of the Landwehrkanal in Kreuzberg. The morning drizzle had evaporated into a luminous white glare that glazed over brick facades and weathered iron balconies.

Without a fixed reservation or timetable, we let the quiet resonance of turntable needles guide our footsteps through an unassuming residential archway. Inside, rows of uncatalogued ECM records leaned against lime-washed plaster walls, smelling faintly of roasted Ethiopian beans and damp paper sleeves.
