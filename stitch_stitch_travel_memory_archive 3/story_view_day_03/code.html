We Got Lost in Berlin

It was around two in the afternoon when our planned route dissolved. Leaving the neoclassical columns of Pariser Platz behind, the yellow M29 tram carried us south into Kreuzberg.

In a hidden brick courtyard scented with roasted arabica and aged vinyl, an unplanned detour became the truest memory of the trip. The city traded its monumental scale for intimate alcoves: damp Ivy clinging to pre-war facades, damp cobblestones gleaming in the brief spring breaks between overcast skies, and the hum of bicycle spokes rolling down Maybachufer.
